1. Academic & Research Informational Purpose

Juris is designed strictly as an automated research tool to assist legal practitioners, law students, academic researchers, corporate compliance officers, and the general public in navigating the body of Philippine statutory law, executive issuances, and Supreme Court jurisprudence.

All materials, synthesized briefs, statutory excerpts, case digests, and legislative updates provided by Juris are for educational, informational, and academic reference only. They must not be relied upon as a definitive substitute for professional legal counsel or formal legal advice tailored to specific factual situations.

2. No Practice of Law & Non-IBP Status

The operators, contributors, and artificial intelligence models of Juris:

  • Are not licensed legal practitioners or a law office authorized to practice law in the Republic of the Philippines under the Rules of Court.
  • Do not render professional legal opinions governed by Canon II of the Code of Professional Responsibility and Accountability (CPRA).
  • Do not represent clients before judicial, quasi-judicial, or administrative tribunals.

If you require legal representation, formal advice on rights or liabilities, or assistance drafting pleadings for court submission, you must consult a qualified, licensed member of the Integrated Bar of the Philippines (IBP).

3. Retrieval Grounding & Verification of Primary Authorities

Juris utilizes a dual dense and sparse (BM25) vector retrieval pipeline with cross-encoder re-ranking against an indexed database of 113,010 official Philippine legal records (spanning Republic Acts, Supreme Court Decisions 1901–2024, Executive Orders, Proclamations, and BetterGov.ph 20th Congress bills).

While Juris incorporates automated two-stage claim verification and Levenshtein quote matching to prevent hallucinations:

  • Users are strongly advised to independently verify all statutory numbers, section numbers, G.R. numbers, and quoted texts with official repositories, including the Official Gazette of the Republic of the Philippines (officialgazette.gov.ph), the Supreme Court E-Library (elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph), or primary published court records.
  • Jurisprudence doctrines may evolve, be modified, or be overturned by subsequent Supreme Court En Banc rulings. Users are responsible for confirming the currency of cited precedents (*Shepardizing* / checking subsequent case history).

4. Third-Party Data Sources & Congress API

Legislative bill data and tracking for the 20th Congress of the Philippines are queried via the BetterGov.ph Open Congress API. Juris does not control, alter, or guarantee the real-time availability or completeness of third-party government feeds. Official bill filings and status should be verified through the official websites of the Senate of the Philippines (senate.gov.ph) and the House of Representatives (congress.gov.ph).
